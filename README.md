# Digital Exclusion of Traditional Textile Weavers in Rural India

## Research Overview
This research explores how digital exclusion impacts traditional textile weavers in rural India, focusing on barriers such as:

- Digital illiteracy
- Lack of internet access
- Infrastructure gaps
- Economic challenges

## Key Findings
- Over 70% of rural weavers lack digital literacy
- Nearly 60% lack reliable internet access
- Heavy dependence on intermediaries reduces profits

## Proposed Solution
Community-Centric Digital Empowerment Model:
- Digital literacy programs
- Community digital hubs
- E-commerce integration
- Sustainable business practices
